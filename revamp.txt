Validation
- for gen 4 games, 
    - look for magic numbers in footer
    - verify the correctness of checksum in footer
- for gen 5 games,
    - verify correctness of checksums at both original + mirror locations
    - verify correctness of checksum block at the very end
- either throw error, or return a savefile interface-compliant object
